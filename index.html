How It Works

Unfold. Strap in. Roll.

From trunk to vet door in under a minute.

1Unfold with one hand

Lift it out of the trunk and pull it open. The frame locks into place on its own.

2Strap in the carriers

Set your cats’ carriers on the padded upper and lower decks and cinch the straps. No wobble.

3Roll in like you own the place

Glide across the lot, set the brake at the desk, and fold it back down when you’re done.

Engineered Details

Every detail earns its place.

Padded, non-slip upper and lower platforms with raised edges and soft-touch straps. Your cats ride in the carriers you already use — the way vets want them to arrive.

FAQ

Good questions.

Are cat carriers included?

No. The Cat Caddy is the stroller only. It’s designed to hold the standard hard-shell carriers most cat owners already have for vet visits.

How many cats can I bring?

Two carriers — one on the lower deck and one on the upper deck.

Will it fit in my car?

It folds flat with one hand and is sized for SUV and most sedan trunks. Exact folded dimensions are listed on the product page.

When will my order ship?

The Cat Caddy is in its first production run. Pre-orders ship in the order they’re received, and we’ll email you a tracking number the day yours leaves.

What if I don’t love it?

Return it within 60 days of delivery for a full refund. Shipping is free both ways in the continental US.
