FAQ

Questions? We’ve got answers.

When will The Cat Caddy be available?

We’re currently in the design and prototyping phase. Waitlist members will be the first to know when pre-orders open. Sign up above to stay in the loop.

What size carriers does it fit?

The Cat Caddy is designed for standard hard-shell carriers (the kind most people already own for vet visits). The platform accommodates 2–3 carriers on two tiers with adjustable straps.

How much weight can it hold?

The target weight capacity is generous enough for multiple cats plus carriers. Final specs will be confirmed after prototype testing and published before pre-orders open.

Does it fold for storage?

Yes. The X-frame design collapses with one hand and stands upright on its own when folded. It fits in an SUV trunk, a closet, or behind a door.

Is this just a fancy shopping cart?

Not even close. The Cat Caddy is purpose-built for cat carriers — with suspension wheels for smooth rolling, padded anti-slip platforms, carrier straps, an ergonomic 5-foot handle, a hand brake, and an integrated console. It’s a mobility solution, not a utility cart.

Will there be color options?

The launch model features our signature royal purple with chrome accents. Additional colorways (Jet Black, Coastal Cream, Forest Green, Blush Sand) are planned for the Luxe model.
