How it works

Bring any carrier. Strap it in. Go.

Each tier is a padded, non-slip tray with a low lip that keeps a carrier centered. Set down any carrier up to 19 by 13 inches, pull two leather straps over the top and click the buckles. Ours fit perfectly, and so does the one already in your closet.

  1. I

    Set it down

    Place the carrier on the padded tray. The raised lip and non-slip surface keep it from sliding.

  2. II

    Strap and click

    Pull both straps over the top and click each buckle. One pull cinches it snug.

  3. III

    Release and lift

    Flip the buckles open and lift. Your cat goes straight into the vet's office in their own carrier.

FAQ

Is it safe for nervous cats?

The ride is cushioned by spring suspension, each cat has its own tier, and Cady carriers include an interior harness tether. If your cat is very anxious, ask the Concierge and we will share what helps.

When does it ship?

We're finishing the design now, then building and testing prototypes. Reservation holders hear about each milestone first and get first access to the first production run.

Do I pay anything to reserve?

No. A reservation saves your design and your place in line. You decide whether to order when orders open.

Will my current cat carrier fit?

Yes. Each tier takes any carrier or crate up to 19 by 13 inches, hard-shell or soft-sided, held by two cross straps with buckles. Cat Cady carriers are designed to match the frame, but you don't need them.

How big can my cats be?

We're designing each carrier for cats up to about 20 lb. We'll confirm the final rating after testing.

Can I get three tiers?

We're launching with two. If you'd want a third, mention it when you reserve. Enough requests and we'll build it.

Can I change my design later?

Yes. Your reservation is a starting point, and you can change any option before you order.
