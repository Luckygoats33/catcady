Built For Vet Day

Designed around the trip, not the shelf.

Most pet strollers are built for walking dogs. The Cat Caddy is built for the one trip every cat owner dreads.

Your cats stay in their carriers

Vets want cats to arrive in a closed carrier, and two cats shouldn’t have to share a compartment. The Cat Caddy holds each carrier on its own padded, non-slip deck with soft straps cinched over the top.

  • Fits standard hard-shell carriers
  • Raised deck edges stop sliding
  • Upper deck adjusts or lifts out

Smooth over curbs and parking lots

10″ white-wall wheels on coil-spring suspension, with swivel fronts for tight clinic hallways. No tires to pump.

Brake, cup holders, storage — right at your hands

A tall, adjustable handle with a vegan-leather grip and a one-touch hand brake that locks the rear wheels at the check-in desk. The molded console holds two drinks plus keys, phone and paperwork under a lid.

One-hand fold for the trunk or closet

Squeeze, fold, done. It stands upright on its own and slides into an SUV or most sedan trunks.

FAQ

Before you buy.

Are cat carriers included?

No — The Cat Caddy is the stroller only. It holds the standard hard-shell carriers most cat owners already have.

Will my carriers fit?

Each deck is sized for a standard hard-shell cat carrier. Measure your carriers’ footprint and compare it to the deck size in the specs above. Questions? Email us and we’ll check for you.

Can I use it with just one cat?

Yes. Use the lower deck on its own, or lift the upper deck out for a lower, more compact profile.

When does my pre-order ship?

Pre-orders ship with our first production run, in the order they’re placed. We’ll email updates along the way and tracking the day it ships.

Can I cancel a pre-order?

Yes — any time before it ships, for a full refund.

What’s your return policy?

60 days from delivery, full refund, free return shipping in the continental US.

Do you ship outside the US?

Not yet. We’re launching in the US first.
